Moon Garden — Rabbit Candy is a skill-based sweet-sorting puzzle: on a 5×4 tray you match berry, mint, honey, and lilac candies into the matching baskets across twelve stages, building streaks for higher scores while three hearts punish wrong baskets. Clearing a stage banks your full round score plus a stage bonus and unlocks a new Garden Light (from Berry Lamp to Moon Gate) in the Garden Book; failing out of hearts still cash out at half the round score with no decoration. There are no in-app purchases—progress, best streak, and garden lights come only from play—and luck barely enters because each stage is built solvable with fixed quotas, so wins hinge on careful sorting and charged rabbit helpers rather than chance.
